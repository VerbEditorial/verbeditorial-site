01. About

I've been cutting since 1987, and I'm still curious about where the story is hiding.

Brand films, documentaries and corporate work for clients like AT&T, Anheuser-Busch InBev, Dell, Edward Jones and John Deere. I co-founded Coolfire Studios, and today I run Verb Editorial, a small studio where the edit leads and the design, motion and finishing follow it.

I work with each client in phases. We figure out what you need to say and who you need to reach, then we build the story that says it.

02. Services
V1 · Editorial

Editorial

Storytelling for short and long form: brand films, documentaries, corporate and campaign work. Structure, pacing and performance, shaped with you from the first assembly.

V2 · Motion

Motion graphics

Titles, typography and animation that carry the same look and rhythm as the cut, so the graphics feel like part of the film instead of a layer on top.

V3 · Design

Design

Visual identity for a piece or a series. It starts with listening: who the audience is, what they need to feel, and what the story has to do.

V4 · AI

AI

Generated footage, voices and tools built into the workflow where they help: faster versions, new looks, fewer limits on a budget. An editor's judgment still decides what serves the story.
